\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

I would like to devote a small space in this thesis to something of great value. I wish to express my appreciation and gratitude to all the people who have accompanied and supported me throughout this work and, more generally, throughout this Master's programme: an important chapter of my life that has lasted almost five years.

This journey, and all the experiences that have accompanied it, were made possible above all by my parents, who supported me and believed in me until the very end. Thank you for giving me the opportunity to study and for always being there for me, even during the most difficult moments. A special thank you also goes to my two dear brothers, who shared these years with me and have always stood by my side.

My most heartfelt thanks go to my partner in life, who has been by my side from the very beginning. To her, who would come to Zurich just to spend time with me, who supported me through the most difficult moments, and who filled these years with wonderful memories. To her, who became my wife before this journey came to an end. Thank you for sharing every moment with me, for always believing in me, and for making this long journey so much more beautiful and joyful.

I must also thank my study group: a group of friends that came together right at the beginning of this journey. You shared with me all the new emotions and experiences that shaped our years of study, and only you truly know what it meant to make it all the way to the end. Thank you for all the days we spent together, for the laughter, and for managing to make even the exam periods a little more enjoyable.

Thank you to all the friends who came bouldering with me. You made my weeks more enjoyable and shared with me a passion that has accompanied me throughout all these years.

Thank you to all my special friends who have been by my side since secondary school. What has grown between us is something unique and irreplaceable. The fact that, despite the passing years, we have never stopped growing and maturing as friends is proof of that. Thank you all for every conversation and for all the support you have given me.

Finally, I would like to thank Achilleas for giving me the opportunity to work on such an ambitious project. Thank you for your trust, for always being available, and for guiding me throughout the development of this thesis, while at the same time giving me the freedom to find my own solutions and make this project my own.
